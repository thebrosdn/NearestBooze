# NearestBooze — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-10-05**

This is the privacy policy for the NearestBooze mobile app. The same text is
available inside the app under **Privacy Policy**.

## What we collect

Nothing. NearestBooze has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics. We do not
operate a server and we never receive, store, or sell your personal data.

## How your location is used

Your device location is read only while the app is open and is used entirely on
your device to sort nearby venues and aim the compass needle. It is never stored
and never leaves the app except as described below.

## Third-party venue lookups

To find nearby venues the app sends your approximate coordinates to the public
[OpenStreetMap Overpass API](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Overpass_API).
Those volunteer-run servers may log the request and your IP address under their
own privacy policies. No identifier of you or your device is attached to the
request.

## Compass data

Magnetometer and heading readings are used on-device only and are never
transmitted.

## Crash reports

If crash reporting is enabled in a release, anonymous crash diagnostics (device
model, OS version, stack trace) are sent to [Sentry](https://sentry.io). Location
data and personally identifying information are explicitly excluded
(`sendDefaultPii: false`).

## Children and alcohol

NearestBooze surfaces alcohol-related venues and is intended for users who are of
legal drinking age in their country. Please drink responsibly and never drink and
drive.

## Changes to this policy

Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date above and shipped in
a new app release.
